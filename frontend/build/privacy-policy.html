1. Introduction

Welcome to Eximpo Global ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and business details
  • Profile Information: Business type, industry, registration numbers, tax IDs
  • Contact Information: Shipping addresses, billing addresses
  • Payment Information: Payment card details (processed securely through our payment provider Razorpay)

2.2 Business Information

  • Product listings and catalogs
  • RFQ (Request for Quotation) submissions and responses
  • Order history and transaction details
  • Communication with other users on the platform

2.3 Technical Information

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Log data (IP address, browser type, access times)
  • Usage data (features used, pages viewed, actions taken)
  • Location data (with your permission)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide and maintain our B2B marketplace services
  • Account Management: To create and manage your account
  • Transaction Processing: To process payments and fulfill orders
  • Communication: To send updates, notifications, and respond to inquiries
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage and improve our services
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and regulations

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Account data is retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for legal and business purposes.
